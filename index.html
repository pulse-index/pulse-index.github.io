ABOUT

Pulse Index

Pulse Index is an artist-led platform for experimental audiovisual art, bringing together sound, moving image, performance, spatial experience and technology. It creates opportunities for artists and organisations working across these fields to meet, exchange knowledge, and develop new collaborations and deeper interconnection — through performances, workshops and digital activities.

The programme includes audiovisual performances in conventional and non-conventional spaces, artist-led workshops, collaborative development sessions such as sound and experimental media demos, and the digital documentation of ephemeral and improvised performances.

Pulse Index builds on existing relationships with independent cultural spaces, organisations and artist networks in Helsinki, while continuing to develop new collaborations. It is a space where artists working across disciplines can experiment, share knowledge and find new points of connection — with the aim of sustaining fair working conditions for everyone involved.
